# Madras GTM Hackathon

A GTM Labs build weekend in Chennai: companies bring real go-to-market problems, teams build working answers in a weekend.

- 4 sponsor tracks, one real problem per track
- Saturday 2 PM to 6 PM: kickoff, sponsor problem pitches, first build sprint
- Sunday 10 AM to 6 PM: build day, 3 PM freeze, live demos and judging, winners by 6 PM
- Judging: 30% solves the sponsor's problem, 30% working demo, 25% GTM insight, 15% pitch and experience

Date, venue, prizes, and sponsor companies are to be confirmed and will be added to the site once confirmed.

This is the sponsor-led GTM edition. The civic edition of Hack for Humanity keeps its own repo
(karanbalaji/Chennai-hack-for-humanity) and site, and will return when the civic edition runs.
